Private login works again after UIS stopped forwarding a silent SSO authorisation to the login form (since 2026-11-25 the callback carries `error=access_denied` and returns 401): the CLI opens the SSO login form once and submits the captcha form there. After a failed automatic refresh, private reads skip it for 10 minutes (`auto_refresh.outcome` `skipped_recent_failure`) so repeated reads no longer spend credential-vault reads and paid captchas; an explicit `auth refresh` always runs and clears the back-off. A credential_process failure now reports `details.stage` `credential_process` instead of looking like a UIS login refusal.
